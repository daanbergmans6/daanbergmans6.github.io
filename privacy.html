Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Hasta Pesto ("the app", "we") collects on iOS and Android, why, and the choices you have.

1. No account, no personal details

You can play without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your progress (levels, broccoli, unlocked characters, settings) is saved only on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app can show an optional rewarded video ad when you choose to revive. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
  • approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads and with the app;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information.

This data is not linked to your identity by us. On iOS, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; you can change this at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (broccoli packs and the premium character) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded so we can deliver it.

4. Google Play Games (Android only)

On Android the app may sign you in to Google Play Games. Google shares your Play Games player profile (gamer name, avatar and player ID) with the app. This is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and your rights

Game progress stays on your device until you delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Contact us and we will help, or use the controls described above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
