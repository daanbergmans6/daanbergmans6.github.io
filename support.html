Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Run away from the angry pasta! Press the UP button to jump (press again in the air to double jump), the DOWN button to slide under obstacles, and collect broccoli. Watch out: in some levels the buttons get swapped for a while.

How do I unlock characters?

Collect broccoli to unlock Gillessen (10,000), Mallebabbe (15,000) and Fristiano (25,000). Lionel is a one-time purchase, and Vivijane unlocks when you finish all levels.

I bought something but didn't get it.

Restart the game with an internet connection; pending purchases are delivered on the next start. To get the premium character back on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the shop. For refunds, contact Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
